# DSA practice workspace

This repository is dedicated to practicing data structures and algorithms.
Prioritize the learner's understanding and independent problem solving over
finishing exercises for them.

## Default: hints first

- Start from the learner's current attempt and explanation when available.
- Give one small, actionable hint or guiding question at a time, then let the
  learner try it. Increase specificity when they ask for more help.
- Begin with observations, small examples, edge cases, or questions about an
  invariant. Avoid immediately revealing the key trick, full algorithm,
  solution-shaped pseudocode, or complete implementation.
- For debugging and reviews, point to the relevant behavior and a small failing
  example or tracing exercise before proposing a fix. Do not rewrite the answer.
- Do not fill in starter code, edit exercise implementations, or add solution
  files unless the learner explicitly requests implementation changes.
- Do not look up or open other completed solutions to an unfinished exercise
  unless the learner asks for that comparison.

## When more direct help is appropriate

- When the learner says they are stuck, provide a more explicit explanation or
  step-by-step walkthrough appropriate to their request. Do not insist on an
  endless sequence of hints.
- When they explicitly ask for a solution, provide it with reasoning and time
  and space complexity. Writing it into the repository still requires a request
  to edit the implementation.
- When they have solved the problem and request another perspective, freely
  discuss alternative approaches, correctness, tradeoffs, and optimizations.

These learning constraints apply to exercises. Carry out requested workspace
configuration, tooling, and documentation work normally.

## Refresh round

- On `practice/refresh`, use the existing problem starter subfolders linked in
  `PROGRESS.md`. Keep one implementation per exercise in its starter file;
  do not recreate solved copies directly under the week folders.
- Preserve the supplied starter contents unless the learner asks to change them.
- `main` and `.practice-archive/` contain previous work. Do not read their
  implementations unless the learner explicitly requests a comparison.
- Track practice in `PROGRESS.md`. Do not mark an exercise Solved without
  evidence from the learner or validation; initial status is Not started.
